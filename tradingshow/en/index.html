About the event

The Bybit Trading Show brought the crypto community together at a night club in Palermo, Buenos Aires, on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. For one night, trading left the screens and became a shared experience, with a live simulated-trading qualifier and a final with prizes.

98attendees

8finalists on stage

How it worked

Each attendee connected to the show app with an NFC wristband and was assigned to one of the teams: Alpha, Bulls, Edge or Scalpers. Participants competed in a simulated-trading qualifier with rankings on LED screens, and 8 finalists played for the prizes on stage with a live host and heart-rate readings: an iPhone for first place, an iPad for second and 100 USDT for six finalists.

A brand in every interaction

The brand wall invited attendees to leave their signature, with interviews and testimonials from community creators, gifts for interactions and a uniformed team. Produced by Tangram for Bybit LATAM, with the World Series of Trading (WSOT) on screens and in the host's mentions.
